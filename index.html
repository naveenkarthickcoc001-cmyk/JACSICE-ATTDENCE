⚡ How It Works

From zero to attendance in 3 steps

SmartAttend makes it incredibly simple for teachers and students alike.

1

Teacher Creates Session

Teacher opens the QR generator, selects the class and time window, and clicks Generate. A unique QR code appears instantly.

2

Students Scan QR

Students open SmartAttend on their phone and scan the QR code displayed in class. GPS and time are verified automatically.

3

Admin Reviews Dashboard

Attendance is recorded in real-time. Admins and teachers can view reports, export data, and identify at-risk students.
