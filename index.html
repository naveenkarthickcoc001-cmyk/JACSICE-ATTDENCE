✨ Features

Everything your college needs,
out of the box

No setup headache. Just sign up and start managing attendance with powerful tools built for real classrooms.

📱
QR Code Attendance
Generate unique QR codes per session. Students scan to mark themselves present — works on any smartphone in seconds.
📊
Real-Time Dashboard
Live counters, department-wise breakdowns, at-risk alerts, and daily summaries — all updating without a page refresh.
✍️
Manual Attendance
Take roll-call manually for each class with one-click present/absent/late marking and bulk-save for entire sections.
📝
IAT Marks Management
Record IAT 1, IAT 2, Model Exam and Semester marks. Instantly see averages, pass/fail stats, and student progress.
📈
Reports & Analytics
Export attendance and marks as CSV. View trend charts, department comparisons, and defaulter lists with one click.
🔐
Firebase Auth
Secure login with Email & Password or passwordless Email Link. Role-based access keeps each user in their lane.
🏫
Multi-Department Support
Manage CSE, ECE, IT, MBA and more simultaneously. Section-wise controls, HOD assignments, and per-dept timetables.
📅
Timetable & Schedule
Build the weekly bell schedule, map teachers to subjects and periods, and auto-link attendance sessions to timetable slots.
✅
Approval Workflow
New students and teachers register and wait for admin approval. Admins get a badge count and approve/reject with one click.
⚡ Quick Start

Up and running in 4 steps

No complex installation. Just register, verify, and go.

1
Register Your College
Admin registers with a college code. Sets up departments, sections, and bell periods in minutes.
2
Invite Teachers & Students
Teachers and students self-register. Admin approves each request from the Approvals page.
3
Mark Attendance Daily
Generate a QR code or use manual roll-call. Students are marked Present, Absent, or Late instantly.
4
View Reports & Marks
Access live dashboards, export CSVs, enter IAT marks, and generate defaulter reports any time.
